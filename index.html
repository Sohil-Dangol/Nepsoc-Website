Who We Are

We are the Warwick Nepalese Society — a welcoming community where Nepali students from all years, alumni, and anyone interested in Nepali culture can come together.

Whether you're looking to celebrate traditions, meet new people, or experience Nepali culture, NepSoc provides a place to build friendships and create unforgettable memories throughout your time at Warwick.

What we Do?

We regularly host a variety of fun and engaging events throughout the year, bringing together students from all backgrounds to celebrate culture, friendship, and community. From Arts and Crafts and cultural activities to Hot Ones challenges, Bar Crawls, and Momo Nights, our events provide the perfect opportunity to meet new people, share experiences, and create lasting memories. Whether you are new to Warwick or a familiar face, everyone is welcome.
